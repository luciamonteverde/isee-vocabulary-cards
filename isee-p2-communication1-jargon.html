Jargon

JAR-gun -- two syllables, stress on the first syllable, JAR. noun

Hear It
What It Means

Jargon is the specialized vocabulary of a group, hard for outsiders to understand.

Show it in context

The skateboarders' jargon -- 'goofy,' 'ollie,' 'sketchy landing' -- left the new kid completely lost.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

On her first hospital shift, the new nurse hears: 'Bed six is NPO pre-op, get vitals q4 and flag the attending stat.' She understands perhaps four of those words. By her third week, she is saying the same sentence herself without a flicker of thought -- and watching a visitor's face go blank at it.

The hospital's abbreviations are jargon: the specialized vocabulary of one profession. The worked example shows both of jargon's faces -- inside the group it is lightning-fast shorthand that saves lives and time; outside the group it is a locked door. That outsiders-shut-out effect is why the word usually carries a faint frown.

rootjargonthe twittering of birds; meaningless chatter (Old French jargoun, probably imitative of bird sounds)

Jargon began in Old French as the word for the TWITTERING OF BIRDS -- busy, chattering sound that carries no meaning to the listener. That is precisely how specialist talk strikes an outsider: rapid, confident, and incomprehensible. The word is probably imitative (built to sound like the chatter it names), and since echo-words leave no clean paper trail, its origin is marked uncertain.

Use the root in a new word: gibberish

The scrambled voicemail came out as pure gibberish.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

Jargon is the insider vocabulary of a profession or group -- legal jargon, medical jargon, tech jargon -- and it usually carries a mild complaint: the word is chosen when the specialized language is shutting somebody out. Inside the group, jargon is genuinely useful shorthand; the criticism lands when it leaks into writing meant for everyone. Good writers translate jargon into plain English; careless ones hide behind it.
