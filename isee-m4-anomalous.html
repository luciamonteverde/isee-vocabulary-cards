Anomalous

/uh-NOM-uh-lus/ — stress on NOM · adjective

REVIEW (Novelty, Strangeness & Variety)

Hear It
What It Means

Something that is anomalous is different from what is normal or expected, standing out as strange or irregular.

Show it in context

One anomalous test result out of a thousand made the engineers rerun the whole batch.

See How It Works · follow the thinking, then try one

Here's how to decide when anomalous is the exact word.

Worked Example

The technician isolated seven anomalous voltage readings from the power grid where equipment should draw steady current.

Anomal- (from anomaly, meaning a deviation from the expected pattern) + -ous (full of, possessing the quality) = full of pattern-breaking. The y of anomaly drops before -ous. Because these readings violated the predicted behavior, anomalous is precise—they don't just measure wrong; they break the established pattern.

Your Turn

Every instrument in the laboratory was calibrated correctly and produced the predicted measurements.

Would anomalous describe these measurements?

Yes

Not quite — It is tempting because laboratory measurements sound technical, and anomalous is a common science word. But every instrument was calibrated correctly and produced the predicted results, so nothing broke the expected pattern.

No

Correct — These results follow the expected pattern exactly, so they are normal, not anomalous. Anomalous means full of pattern-breaking, the opposite of what happened here.

Synonyms · words that mean the same — tap one to see how it differs
abnormal

Not conforming to a standard or norm. But abnormal just means 'not normal' without implying the meaningful pattern-breaking that anomalous emphasizes.

atypical

Not typical of a kind or group. Atypical is gentler—it suggests something outside the usual type, while anomalous implies the deviation is notable or scientifically significant.

irregular

Lacking uniformity or consistency. Irregular focuses on unevenness, while anomalous focuses on deviating from an expected baseline or established pattern.

Antonyms · opposites

normal — The expected or standard state. Since anom- means away from the norm, anomalous is full of being away from normal.

Build the Word · the heart of this unit

anomal(y) + -ous

anomaly (something that breaks the pattern) + -ous (full of) = full of pattern-breaking; the y of anomaly drops before the suffix. If you know anomaly, you already own this word.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

neutral to formal

Reach for anomalous when something deviates from an expected pattern in a way that merits investigation or explanation—especially in scientific, technical, or analytical contexts. It suggests the deviation is meaningful and surprising, not just unusual.
