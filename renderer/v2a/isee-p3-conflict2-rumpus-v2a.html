What It Means

Rumpus is a noisy disturbance with a playful, almost comic flavor, loud but usually minor and harmless.

See the word in context

The escaped goat caused quite a rumpus in the middle of the farmers' market.

A rumpus broke out in the bleachers the moment the umpire reversed his call.

This wasn't a quiet disagreement -- it was a loud, chaotic outburst all at once, which is exactly a rumpus.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    commotion: A commotion is any burst of noisy, confused activity -- a scuffle, a spill, a sudden crowd. A rumpus is a commotion with a playful, almost comic flavor: the word itself sounds like mischief, and it usually names trouble that is loud but harmless.

    uproar: An uproar is the roar a crowd sends up in reaction -- people shouting in anger, protest, or wild excitement about something. A rumpus is the disturbance itself rather than the reaction to it, and it stays smaller and lighter: closer to mischief than to outrage.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    tranquility: a state of calm and quiet

    Where it sits · how wild and dangerous the disturbance

    noise → rumpus → riot

    noise vs. rumpus: Noise is any unwanted sound; a rumpus is specifically a chaotic, noisy disturbance involving people or animals.

    riot vs. rumpus: A riot is violent, destructive, and far more dangerous than a rumpus, which is a loud but usually harmless disturbance.
