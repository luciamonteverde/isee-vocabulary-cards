Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Egalitarian is relating to the belief that all people deserve equal rights and treatment.

See the word in context

The founders designed an egalitarian classroom where every student, regardless of background, had the same voice in discussions.

The new company is deliberately egalitarian: the intern and the chief executive eat at the same tables, receive the same benefits, and cast equal votes on office policy.

The workplace is built on the principle that everyone deserves the same rights and treatment. Egalitarian describes a belief or system committed to equality -- not just a single act of fairness in one moment.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    equitable: Equitable means fair in result -- each side gets what its needs call for, even if the shares differ. Egalitarian insists on identical, equal treatment for everyone, as a matter of principle. An equitable judge may divide unevenly to be fair; an egalitarian rule gives everyone exactly the same.

    inclusive: Inclusive means taking care that no one is left out -- every voice invited into the room. Egalitarian goes further than the invitation: an inclusive club may still keep ranks and titles inside it, while an egalitarian one treats every member's rights and standing as exactly equal.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    elitist: believing a select few deserve special status or treatment

    discriminatory: treating certain people or groups unfairly

    Where it sits · how far the fairness principle extends

    unbiased → egalitarian → democratic

    unbiased vs. egalitarian: Unbiased is smaller in scope than egalitarian -- a single referee can be unbiased in one game, while egalitarian names a whole principled commitment that all people deserve equal rights and treatment.

    democratic vs. egalitarian: Democratic can reach even further than egalitarian: an egalitarian outlook holds that everyone deserves equal treatment, while a fully democratic system builds that belief into its very structure, giving every member an equal voice in every decision.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Egalitarian describes beliefs, systems, and societies committed to equal rights: an egalitarian policy, an egalitarian constitution, an egalitarian marriage. Do not use it as a fancy word for 'nice' or 'fair' in a single moment -- it names a principle of equality, not one kind gesture.
