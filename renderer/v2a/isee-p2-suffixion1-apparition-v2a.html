What It Means

Apparition is a ghost or ghost-like figure -- a supernatural sudden appearance that actually shows itself to someone.

See the word in context

The night watchman swore he saw an apparition drifting through the abandoned hallway just before the lights flickered out.

Visitors to the old lighthouse often report an apparition of a woman in a long coat, standing at the top of the stairs and staring out to sea.

An apparition is specifically a ghostly or supernatural-seeming figure -- not just any surprising sight.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    phantom: A phantom is a ghost, or more broadly anything you seem to perceive that is not really there -- phantom pain, a phantom traffic jam. An apparition is an actual sighting: a ghostly or supernatural figure that shows itself to someone.

    specter: A specter is a ghost, but the word now does most of its work on feared possibilities -- people speak of the specter of famine or defeat. An apparition must actually appear and be seen; a specter can frighten everyone without ever showing itself.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    reality: something that actually, tangibly exists

    substance: solid, real, physical matter

    Where it sits · how intense the eerie presence is

    shadow → apparition → haunting

    shadow vs. apparition: A shadow is far fainter than an apparition -- a dim, half-seen shape that may be nothing supernatural at all, while an apparition is an unmistakable ghostly figure that shows itself.

    haunting vs. apparition: A haunting is a persistent, unsettling supernatural presence, even more intense than a single apparition.
