What It Means

Nihilistic is believing that life is meaningless and that traditional values or beliefs are unfounded.

See the word in context

After the company's collapse, his nihilistic outlook convinced him that no amount of hard work ever really mattered.

The film's nihilistic antihero insists that nothing he does -- good or bad -- has any lasting meaning in an indifferent universe.

Nihilistic describes a belief that life, morals, or meaning itself are all essentially empty or nonexistent.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    despairing: Despairing means all hope is gone -- the person still cares desperately about the outcome but sees no way it can come right. Nihilistic runs deeper and colder: it denies that outcomes, values, or meaning exist to hope for at all.

    fatalistic: Fatalistic means believing every outcome was settled in advance, so effort and precaution change nothing. A fatalist may still think life has meaning -- it is simply out of our hands -- while nihilistic denies that anything means anything at all.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    hopeful: having a feeling of optimism about the future

    purposeful: having a clear, meaningful goal or intention

    Where it sits · how completely meaning is denied

    skeptical → nihilistic → misanthropic

    skeptical vs. nihilistic: Skeptical is the mild end of this scale -- a skeptical person doubts particular claims but still assumes some things are true and matter, while nihilistic denies that anything has meaning at all.

    misanthropic vs. nihilistic: Misanthropic goes even further, adding an active distrust or hatred of humanity itself to nihilistic despair.

    Word Choice
