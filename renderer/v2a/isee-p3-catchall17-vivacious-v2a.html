vivacious

vih-VAY-shus -- from Latin vivax ('lively'), rooted in vivere, 'to live' (the same root behind vivid and survive).

Meaning

What It Means

Vivacious means attractively lively and full of energy in personality and manner, with a sustained sparkle of charm.

See the word in context

The new tour guide's vivacious energy made even the driest historical facts feel exciting.

Despite being the shortest speaker on the panel, the vivacious young scientist held the entire auditorium's attention with her animated gestures and quick laugh.

Her lively energy and expressive manner -- not her height or her credentials -- are what make her vivacious.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    energetic: Energetic means full of physical energy and drive — an energetic hike, an energetic morning of cleaning. Vivacious is a compliment about charm rather than effort: it names the sparkling, sociable manner that makes a person's company enjoyable.

    spirited: Spirited describes vigor in effort or contest — a spirited defense, a spirited debate, a spirited comeback. Vivacious is social rather than combative: it names the attractive, sparkling liveliness in a person's manner and company.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    listless: lacking energy or enthusiasm

    somber: serious, dark, and joyless in mood

    Where it sits · how much energy spills out

    animated → vivacious → effervescent

    animated vs. vivacious: Animated is milder than vivacious: a puppy hopping in circles at the sight of a leash is animated, but a vivacious person radiates a stronger, more sustained sparkle of energy and charm.

    effervescent vs. vivacious: Effervescent is more intense than vivacious: a vivacious host keeps a party lively, while an effervescent toast bubbles over with energy so extreme the whole reception is laughing and cheering within seconds.

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Vivacious is almost always a compliment about a person's outward energy and charm -- unlike merely 'energetic,' it specifically suggests a lively, sparkling personality that others enjoy being around.
