What It Means

Shrewd means intelligent and perceptive about how things and people really work, turning that reading into practical advantage.

See the word in context

The shrewd farmer sold his hay early, guessing correctly that prices would fall after the rains.

Two museums want the same famous painting on loan. The smaller museum's shrewd director does her homework and discovers the owner's real worry is security -- so instead of offering more money, she offers a state-of-the-art vault and wins the loan.

She is intelligent, clever, and perceptive about how things and people really work. Shrewd judgment reads the situation beneath the surface and turns that reading into practical advantage.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    canny: Canny means clever and careful in practical matters, especially with money -- a canny shopper never overpays. Shrewd reaches further: shrewd judgment reads people and situations beneath the surface, then acts on what it sees to come out ahead.

    astute: Astute means keenly accurate at reading a situation -- an astute observer notices what is really going on beneath appearances. Shrewd adds the practical move that follows: a shrewd person not only reads the moment correctly but turns it to real advantage.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    foolish: showing a lack of good sense

    dim: slow to understand

    Where it sits · how much scheming the cleverness carries

    sharp → shrewd → calculating

    sharp vs. shrewd: Sharp says the mind is quick, while shrewd adds worldly wisdom: a sharp student solves the puzzle fast, but a shrewd one also notices why the puzzle was assigned.

    calculating vs. shrewd: Calculating is the strongest and coldest: a shrewd trader reads people and situations to everyone's benefit, but a calculating one weighs every kindness for what it will earn.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Shrewd praises practical intelligence: a shrewd investor, a shrewd guess, a shrewd judge of character. It sees how the world actually works and acts on it. The praise can cool slightly in context -- a 'shrewd operator' may bend rules -- but on the ISEE, shrewd reads as clever and perceptive, not dishonest.
