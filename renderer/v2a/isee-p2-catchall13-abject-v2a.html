abject

AB-jekt -- Latin ab- ('away') plus jacere ('to throw'): thrown down, cast away, as low as a thing can go.

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Abject is extremely bad and degrading.

See the word in context

The letter was an abject apology, full of shame from the first line to the last.

A champion who has boasted for months about an easy win is knocked out in ninety seconds, and has to walk the whole length of the arena to the locker room in abject defeat, in total silence.

A state so bad that it strips away a person's dignity is what makes the defeat abject. Unlike 'unfortunate,' which is only bad luck, abject adds humiliation to the harm.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    wretched: Wretched describes a state so pitiful that anyone watching feels sorry for the sufferer. It sits just as low as abject, but the two point at different things: wretched stresses the pity a condition stirs in others, while abject stresses the humiliation it forces on the person living it.

    utter: Utter means complete or total, and it works only planted directly in front of its noun -- an utter failure, an utter surprise. It measures how entire something is, while abject measures how low and degrading a state is: an utter collapse is total; an abject one is humiliating.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    comfortable: free from hardship

    dignified: worthy of honor and respect

    Where it sits · how deeply dignity is stripped away

    miserable → abject → groveling

    miserable vs. abject: Miserable is milder than abject -- a miserable day is unpleasant, while abject conditions are bad enough to strip away a person's dignity.

    groveling vs. abject: Groveling goes a step past abject: an abject apology is full of shame, while a groveling one throws dignity away completely, begging on its knees for forgiveness.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Abject keeps a small set of favorite companions: abject poverty, abject failure, abject misery, an abject apology. In every one, it means the thing is as low and humiliating as it could possibly be.
