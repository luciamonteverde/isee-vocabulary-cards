effrontery

eh-FRUN-tuh-ree

What It Means

Effrontery is shameless, unapologetic boldness -- the nerve to do something disrespectful or inappropriate without any embarrassment.

See the word in context

The substitute teacher was stunned by the effrontery of the student who casually put his feet up on the desk during the very first class.

It took real effrontery for the passenger to loudly insist on early boarding after being the last one to arrive at the gate.

Effrontery isn't just rudeness -- it's rudeness delivered without a trace of shame, as if the person genuinely believes they've done nothing wrong.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    audacity: Audacity is boldness itself -- the raw willingness to attempt something risky or startling -- and it can even be admirable. Effrontery is never admirable: it is boldness spent on something disrespectful or inappropriate, carried off without a flicker of embarrassment.

    gall: Gall is the blunt, everyday word for shameless nerve -- he had the gall to cut the line and then demand a refund. Like effrontery it is always disapproving; gall belongs to casual speech, while effrontery is the formal word for truly jaw-dropping disrespect.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    timidity: shyness or a lack of boldness

    humility: a modest, unassuming manner

    Where it sits · how shameless the nerve becomes

    cheekiness → effrontery → impudence

    cheekiness vs. effrontery: Cheekiness is far milder than effrontery -- a cheeky joke winks at the rules playfully, while effrontery tramples them without a flicker of shame.

    impudence vs. effrontery: Impudence pushes even further than effrontery -- it's the same shameless, disrespectful boldness taken to an even greater extreme.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Effrontery almost always describes a specific act -- 'the effrontery to do X' -- rather than a general personality trait; it captures the shock of someone's shameless nerve in the moment.
