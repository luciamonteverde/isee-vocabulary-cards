pinnacle

PIN-uh-kul — think of a mountain's 'pin'-sharp point; the pinnacle is the very top.

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Pinnacle is the most successful point of something; the highest point of achievement, earned rather than climbed.

See the word in context

Winning the Nobel Prize marked the absolute pinnacle of the scientist's forty-year career.

A young violinist practices for fifteen years, wins a string of regional competitions, and finally reaches the pinnacle of her career: performing as a soloist with the world's most famous orchestra in front of a sold-out concert hall.

That career-defining performance, the highest point everything else was building toward, is what pinnacle means. Unlike 'goal,' which is just something aimed for, pinnacle specifically means the very peak of achievement.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    summit: A summit is the literal topmost point of a mountain, reached by climbing -- climbers touch the summit, take a photograph, and descend. Pinnacle long ago moved into figurative territory: the pinnacle of a career or a civilization is an achievement, not a rock. A summit is climbed; a pinnacle is earned.

    crowning achievement: A crowning achievement is the single accomplishment itself -- the prize, the record, the masterpiece -- that tops everything else a person has done. Pinnacle names the position rather than the deed: the highest point of the career or era that such an achievement marks.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    nadir: the lowest point of something

    low point: the worst or least successful moment

    Where it sits · how absolute the high point is

    peak → pinnacle → zenith

    peak vs. pinnacle: Peak is a more everyday, general word than pinnacle -- something can peak often (like traffic or temperature), while pinnacle is reserved for a rare, career-defining high point.

    zenith vs. pinnacle: Zenith can feel even more formal and absolute than pinnacle: pinnacle suggests the top of a climb, but zenith suggests the single highest point possible, with nothing above it.
