valiant

VAL-yunt -- two syllables, stress on VAL.

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Valiant is possessing or showing courage and determination.

See the word in context

The firefighters made a valiant effort to save the old barn, battling the flames until dawn.

Though hopelessly outnumbered by the storm, the valiant lifeboat crew rows straight into the towering waves to reach the stranded sailors.

Facing real danger with courage and determination is exactly what valiant describes. A valiant act does not have to succeed -- what makes it valiant is the bravery and resolve behind it.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    courageous: Courageous means showing strength in the face of fear, and it fits any quiet setting -- a courageous question, a courageous admission. Valiant adds action and determination: a valiant effort presses on against the odds, usually in some visible struggle or fight.

    gallant: Gallant describes brave or noble conduct carried off with style and courtesy -- holding a door with a flourish, offering an arm, keeping perfect manners under pressure. Valiant keeps the spotlight on courage and determination themselves; a valiant attempt needs no fine manners, only the resolve to keep fighting.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    cowardly: lacking the courage to face danger

    fearful: full of fear; easily frightened

    Where it sits · how noble and exceptional the courage is

    brave → valiant → heroic

    brave vs. valiant: Brave is the everyday word -- valiant says more: it adds determination and a touch of nobility, the difference between simply facing danger and fighting on against the odds.

    heroic vs. valiant: Heroic can go a step beyond valiant: a valiant effort is courageous and determined, while heroic suggests courage so exceptional that it makes the person a hero in others' eyes.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Valiant often appears in the phrase 'a valiant effort' or 'a valiant attempt' -- frequently for brave, determined tries that fall just short, as in 'a valiant comeback that ended one point shy.' The courage counts even when the result disappoints.
