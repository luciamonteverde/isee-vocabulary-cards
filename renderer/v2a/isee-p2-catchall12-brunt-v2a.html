brunt

BRUNT -- origin uncertain, perhaps Old Norse for a fiery charge; think of taking the main blow of an attack.

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Brunt is the worst or heaviest part of something.

See the word in context

Coastal villages bore the brunt of the hurricane while inland towns saw only rain.

When the company's plan fails, the executives who designed it stay comfortable upstairs -- but the warehouse workers bear the brunt, losing their overtime, their bonuses, and finally their jobs.

Those workers are absorbing the worst part of the failure -- its main blow. That heaviest share of damage or hardship is the brunt, and 'bear the brunt' is exactly how the word is most often used.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    force: Force measures how hard something hits -- the raw strength of a blow, a wind, an argument. The brunt is not the strength itself but the worst share of it: whoever bears the brunt is the one the force lands on hardest.

    toll: The toll is the total damage counted up afterward -- lives, money, and losses added into one grim sum. The brunt is about distribution instead: the heaviest part of the blow, and who had to absorb it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    fringe: the outer edge, farthest from the center

    periphery: the outer boundary, away from the main action

    Where it sits · how heavy and lasting the blow is

    impact → brunt → burden

    impact vs. brunt: Impact is more neutral than brunt -- every collision has an impact, while the brunt is specifically the worst, heaviest share of it that someone or something absorbs.

    burden vs. brunt: Burden lasts longer than brunt: a burden is a heavy load carried over time, while the brunt is the concentrated main blow of a particular event.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Brunt lives almost entirely inside one phrase: to 'bear the brunt of' something -- a storm, a recession, someone's anger. Learn the phrase and you've learned the word.
