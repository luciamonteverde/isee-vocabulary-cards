What It Means

Riveting means so completely engrossing that attention cannot be pried away.

See the word in context

The documentary about deep-sea volcanoes was so riveting that nobody touched the popcorn.

The librarian opens the book and reads only the first chapter aloud. It is so riveting that when the recess bell rings, not one student moves -- twenty-six pairs of eyes stay locked on her, waiting for the next sentence.

Riveting means so completely engrossing that attention cannot be pried away. The word comes from rivets, the metal pins that permanently fasten steel plates -- a riveting story fastens your attention just as firmly.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    gripping: Gripping holds your attention tightly, and it almost always squeezes with suspense -- a gripping thriller keeps you tense about what happens next. Riveting can fasten you to anything at all, tense or calm: a lecture, a chess match, even a budget meeting.

    engrossing: Engrossing means absorbing all of your attention -- an engrossing puzzle swallows a whole afternoon before you notice. Riveting is that same total capture with force added: it does not just absorb you, it bolts you in place, unable to look away.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    dull: not interesting at all

    monotonous: tiresomely unchanging

    Where it sits · how firmly it holds your attention

    captivating → riveting → mesmerizing

    captivating vs. riveting: Captivating charms you -- a captivating melody makes you want to stay -- while riveting goes further: it pins you in place, unable to look away.

    mesmerizing vs. riveting: Mesmerizing goes past even riveting: a riveting account keeps you alert, pinned to every word, while a mesmerizing one pulls you half into a trance -- you drift with it and lose track of the room.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Riveting praises anything that seizes total attention: a riveting account, testimony, or final set. It is stronger than interesting by a mile -- the image is mechanical, your eyes bolted to the thing -- so save it for moments when attention genuinely cannot wander.
