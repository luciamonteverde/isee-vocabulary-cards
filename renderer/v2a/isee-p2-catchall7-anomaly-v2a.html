anomaly

uh-NOM-uh-lee — 'an-' means not, and the rest relates to 'even/regular'; an anomaly is something not even or regular.

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Anomaly is a thing so far outside the normal pattern that it seems odd or hard to explain.

See the word in context

The scientist noticed a strange anomaly in the data that didn't match any of her predictions.

A doctor reviewing thousands of routine X-rays notices an anomaly on one patient's scan: a small shadow in a spot where nothing should appear at all, unlike any of the hundreds of other scans she's reviewed that week.

That single unexpected result, standing out from an otherwise predictable pattern, is what makes it an anomaly. Unlike 'mistake,' which implies an error was made, anomaly simply means something that doesn't fit the expected pattern.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    irregularity: An irregularity is a failure to follow the proper rule or procedure -- a meeting held without required notice, a payment logged in the wrong ledger. An anomaly is stranger: a result so far off the expected pattern that it resists explanation. Every anomaly is surprising; an irregularity can be perfectly mundane.

    aberration: An aberration is a sharp, troubling departure from what is normal or from someone's usual character, and it often carries a hint of alarm or blame. An anomaly is more neutral and clinical: it simply does not fit the pattern, with no judgment attached.

    discrepancy: A discrepancy is a mismatch between two things that should agree, like two totals that fail to line up. An anomaly needs no second record to disagree with -- it is a single result that falls outside the expected pattern all on its own.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    norm: the usual, typical, or expected standard

    regularity: the quality of following a consistent pattern

    Where it sits · how precise and formal the label is

    oddity → anomaly → deviation

    oddity vs. anomaly: Oddity is a more casual word than anomaly -- an oddity is just a strange curiosity, while anomaly is a more formal, technical term for a deviation from an expected pattern or model.

    deviation vs. anomaly: Deviation can feel more precise than anomaly: anomaly often implies something puzzling or hard to explain, while deviation simply and neutrally measures how far something strays from the norm.
