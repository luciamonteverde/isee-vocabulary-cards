crestfallen

KREST-fah-len

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Crestfallen is sad and visibly deflated right after a specific hope has been dashed.

See the word in context

The team walked off the field crestfallen, having lost the championship game in the final seconds.

Crestfallen after her science fair project was disqualified for a technicality, Priya stared at the empty display table where her volcano model had stood minutes before.

Crestfallen describes the deflated feeling right after hopes are dashed -- a specific moment of letdown, not a general mood.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    dejected: Dejected means in low, discouraged spirits -- worn down and short on hope -- an everyday heaviness that can drag on without one clear cause. Crestfallen is sharper and more specific: the sudden, visible slump at the exact moment one particular hope is dashed.

    downcast: Downcast describes how sadness shows in the body: the lowered eyes and hanging head you can see across a room. Crestfallen adds the story behind that look -- a specific hope has just collapsed, and the person visibly deflates because of it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    overjoyed: filled with great happiness

    jubilant: feeling or expressing great happiness and triumph

    Where it sits · how deep the letdown goes

    disappointed → crestfallen → devastated

    disappointed vs. crestfallen: Disappointed is milder than crestfallen: getting the wrong ice-cream flavor is disappointing and forgotten in five minutes, but a crestfallen person visibly droops under a much heavier letdown.

    devastated vs. crestfallen: Losing a life-changing opportunity can leave someone devastated for a long time, far beyond crestfallen.
