Meaning

What It Means

To propagate is to spread something widely -- gardeners propagate plants by growing new ones from seeds or cuttings, and people propagate ideas by passing them along until they multiply.

See the word in context

Volunteers propagate the endangered wildflowers in greenhouses before replanting them across the prairie.

One student posts the dance challenge on Monday; by Friday it has propagated to forty countries, with kids filming their own versions and passing them along to their followers.

It spread widely as each person passed it to the next. To propagate is to spread and promote something, from plants grown from cuttings to ideas that multiply from person to person.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    circulate: To circulate is to pass something around within a group -- a memo circulates through an office, coins circulate from hand to hand. Propagate goes further: it multiplies what it spreads, growing new copies or converts as it travels, and it often implies deliberate promotion.

    broadcast: To broadcast is to send something out to a wide audience all at once, the way a station beams one signal to everyone. Propagate spreads person by person: each receiver passes it along or grows it anew, rather than everyone getting it in a single blast.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    suppress: to stop something from spreading or being known

    contain: to keep something within limits

    Where it sits · how deliberately it gets passed along

    spread → propagate → disseminate

    spread vs. propagate: Spread is the plain, everyday word and weaker than propagate -- butter spreads on toast, but ideas and plants are propagated: spread deliberately and multiplied as they go.

    disseminate vs. propagate: Disseminate is even more systematic than propagate: to propagate an idea is to help it spread and take root, while to disseminate it is to broadcast it methodically, scattering it like seed to every corner of an audience.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Propagate works in two worlds: gardeners propagate plants from seeds and cuttings, and people propagate ideas, beliefs, and rumors. In the idea sense it is neutral-to-formal and often implies deliberate promotion -- propaganda is its close cousin -- so choose it when something is being actively spread, not just drifting.
