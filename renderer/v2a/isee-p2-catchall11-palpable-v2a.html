PAL-puh-bul -- shares a root with 'palpitate,' meaning to touch or feel.

What It Means

Palpable is able to be touched or felt; so intense it can almost be physically felt.

See the word in context

As the jury filed back into the courtroom, the tension in the room was palpable.

The instant the results are announced, silence grips the entire stadium and the disappointment in the air is so palpable you could almost reach out and touch it.

An emotion so strong it feels almost physical is what makes the disappointment palpable. Unlike 'subtle,' which is easy to miss, palpable is impossible to ignore.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    tangible: Tangible means real enough to touch, count, or measure -- tangible proof, tangible results, tangible assets. Palpable usually describes a mood or emotion so intense it feels almost physical, like relief or tension filling a room. Tangible points to concrete things; palpable points to atmospheres.

    unmistakable: Unmistakable means so clear it cannot be confused with anything else, however you happen to perceive it. Palpable is more specific: a feeling so strong it seems almost physically touchable. A signature can be unmistakable; only an intense atmosphere or emotion is palpable.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    imperceptible: impossible to notice or sense

    faint: so slight it is barely noticed

    Where it sits · how physically real it seems

    evident → palpable → overpowering

    evident vs. palpable: Evident is a bit milder than palpable -- evident just means clearly visible, while palpable adds the sense that a feeling is so strong it seems almost physical.

    overpowering vs. palpable: Overpowering goes further than palpable: a palpable feeling can almost be touched, while an overpowering one presses in until people cannot ignore it or carry on as usual.
