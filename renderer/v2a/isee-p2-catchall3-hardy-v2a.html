hardy

HAR-dee — sounds like 'hearty' but spelled with a d; hardy things are hard to harm.

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Hardy is tough enough to withstand difficult or harsh living conditions over time without being harmed by them.

See the word in context

These hardy little shrubs survive the mountain's freezing winters year after year.

A tiny flower grows on a windswept cliff above the sea. It is blasted by salt spray all summer and buried in frost all winter. Yet, hardy as it is, it blooms again every spring.

It endures harsh conditions year after year and comes back anyway. Unlike 'strong,' which suggests raw power, hardy specifically means able to withstand hardship over time.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    tough: Tough means able to endure rough treatment — being dropped, knocked about, or handled carelessly. Hardy is about enduring a harsh environment: a hardy plant or animal comes through frost, drought, and lean seasons unhurt. Tough survives abuse from outside hands; hardy survives hard living conditions over time.

    rugged: Rugged means sturdy and able to take hard use, and it mostly describes gear — rugged boots, a rugged truck. Hardy mostly describes living things: plants, animals, and people that keep thriving in rough climates. A rugged jacket shrugs off wear; a hardy shrub shrugs off winter.

    durable: Durable means lasting a long time without wearing out, so it praises boots, tools, and fabrics that survive years of use. Hardy praises living things that survive hardship itself — frost, drought, thin soil, hard winters. A durable tent resists wear; a hardy plant resists the weather.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    fragile: easily broken

    delicate: easily harmed or damaged

    Where it sits · how much punishment it can survive

    healthy → hardy → indestructible

    healthy vs. hardy: Healthy is weaker than hardy — a healthy plant is doing well right now, while a hardy one is built to stay well through frost, drought, and abuse.

    indestructible vs. hardy: Indestructible is stronger than hardy: hardy things survive harsh conditions, but indestructible things cannot be destroyed at all.
