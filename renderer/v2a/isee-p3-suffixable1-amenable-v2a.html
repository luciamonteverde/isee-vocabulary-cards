amenable

uh-MEE-nuh-bul

What It Means

Amenable is willing and open to agreeing with a suggestion, plan, or authority; responsive to persuasion.

See the word in context

The two neighboring towns had feuded over the shared water line for a decade, but both new mayors turned out to be far more amenable to compromise than their predecessors had ever been.

Union representatives were pleasantly surprised to find the new management team amenable to flexible scheduling, something the previous owners had refused to even discuss.

Amenable describes a specific willingness to go along with a suggestion or plan -- it's always about being open to something in particular, not a general personality trait.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    agreeable: Agreeable describes a generally easygoing person who tends to go along with most things -- it is a standing personality trait. Amenable is narrower and needs a target: someone is amenable to one particular plan, suggestion, or compromise, whatever their personality is like the rest of the time.

    receptive: Receptive means open to hearing and considering an idea -- a receptive listener takes a proposal seriously and weighs it. Amenable goes a step further: an amenable person is ready to actually agree to the specific plan, not just to think it over.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    obstinate: stubbornly refusing to change one's mind

    unyielding: refusing to give in or compromise

    Where it sits · how ready to go along

    open-minded → amenable → compliant

    open-minded vs. amenable: Open-minded is weaker than amenable: it only means willing to consider new ideas in general, while an amenable person goes further, ready to actually agree to a specific plan.

    compliant vs. amenable: Compliant goes further than amenable -- it means actively following instructions, not just being open to a suggestion.

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Amenable almost always needs a target -- someone is amenable to a plan, a suggestion, or a compromise. It rarely stands alone as a general personality description the way affable or equable can.
