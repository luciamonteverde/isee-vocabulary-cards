prototype

PRO-tuh-type — starts with 'proto,' meaning first; a prototype is the very first typed-out version.

What It Means

Prototype is an original or first working model of something, built to test a design before it is produced in large numbers.

See the word in context

The engineering team built three different prototypes before settling on a final design for the drone.

A shoe company designs a new running shoe. Before ordering a hundred thousand pairs, they build just a handful of prototypes, hand them to test runners, and gather feedback on comfort and durability.

Those few test shoes exist only to check the design before the factory run. Unlike 'sample,' which can just mean any small example pulled from a finished batch, a prototype comes before mass production even begins.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    model: A model is any version built to represent a design — it can be made before, during, or long after the real thing, and at any scale. A prototype is one particular kind of model: the first working version, built before production begins so the design can be tested.

    mockup: A mockup is a full-size stand-in built to show how a design will look; it often does not work at all — a foam phone or a cardboard cockpit is enough. A prototype must actually function, because its whole job is to be tested and improved.

    blueprint: A blueprint is the detailed plan on paper — every measurement drawn, nothing yet built. A prototype is what happens when the plan leaves the page: a real, working first version of the design that testers can hold, run, and try to break.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    replica: an exact copy of a finished original

    finished product: a completed item ready for sale or use

    Where it sits · how finished the design is

    sketch → prototype → production model

    sketch vs. prototype: A sketch is weaker than a prototype — a sketch is just a rough drawing of an idea, while a prototype is an actual built, testable model of the design.

    production model vs. prototype: A production model is a step beyond a prototype: a prototype is built to test and refine a design, but a production model is the finalized version that gets mass-manufactured.
