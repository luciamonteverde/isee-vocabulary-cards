What It Means

To adhere is to stick fast to, or to follow a rule or belief closely.

See the word in context

Pilots must adhere to the checklist before every takeoff, no matter how routine the flight.

The new baker adheres to the hundred-year-old recipe exactly -- same flour, same timings, same folds -- refusing to change a single step no matter what shortcuts others suggest.

To adhere is to follow something closely and faithfully, as if stuck to it -- to stick fast, literally (tape to a wall) or figuratively (a person to a rule, code, or belief).

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    observe: To observe a rule or custom is to keep it dutifully because it stands -- a driver observes the speed limit, a family observes a holiday. Adhere runs deeper: you adhere to rules, codes, or beliefs you feel personally bound to, keeping them exactly and faithfully.

    conform: To conform is to take your cues from the group -- matching how the people around you dress, speak, and act so that you blend in. Adhere points at something fixed: you adhere to a rule, code, or belief, keeping it exactly even when no one else does.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    disregard: pay no attention to

    violate: break a rule or agreement

    Where it sits · how strictly the rule is kept

    follow → adhere → cling

    follow vs. adhere: Follow is casual next to adhere -- you can follow a recipe loosely, but to adhere to it is to stick to every step without deviation.

    cling vs. adhere: Cling goes further than adhere: to adhere to a rule is to keep it faithfully and exactly, while to cling to it is to grip it past all sense -- holding on even after the rule has stopped serving anyone.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Adhere almost always brings 'to' along: adhere to a schedule, adhere to a diet, adhere to one's principles. Use it for faithful, exact following -- and the literal sense is just as strict: stickers adhere to laptops exactly as monks adhere to vows.
