Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

To aspire is to have a great ambition or ultimate goal; to strive toward it.

See the word in context

Many young engineers aspire to design the spacecraft that will one day carry astronauts to Mars.

A ten-year-old tapes a photograph of the Supreme Court above her desk and tells everyone who asks that she aspires to argue a case there someday.

Holding a great, guiding ambition -- and orienting your life toward it -- is what it means to aspire. Unlike a passing wish, an aspiration is a lofty goal that shapes what you work toward every day.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    strive: To strive is to labor hard toward something, straining against difficulty — the word points at the sweat and effort itself. To aspire points at the goal instead: you can quietly aspire to greatness for years, but you strive only while actively working.

    yearn: To yearn is to long deeply for something out of reach — whether or not you can do anything to get it. To aspire is more purposeful: it names a high ambition you orient your work and choices toward.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    settle: to accept something less than one hoped for

    stagnate: to stop developing or making progress

    Where it sits · how lofty the goal is

    aim → aspire → dream

    aim vs. aspire: Aim is flatter and more practical than aspire -- you can aim to finish your homework by six, while to aspire is reserved for lofty, long-range ambitions that shape a whole path.

    dream vs. aspire: Dream reaches even higher than aspire — a dream can float far past any realistic plan, while to aspire means the goal, however lofty, is one you are seriously working toward.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Aspire is nearly always followed by 'to' -- either 'aspire to' plus a verb ('aspire to become a surgeon') or plus a noun ('aspire to greatness'). It is the natural verb for ambition: the goal is high, and the aspirer is determined to climb toward it.
