affinity

uh-FIN-ih-tee — think of 'affinity' as a fine connection you feel toward something.

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Affinity is a natural, lasting liking for something, a pull that keeps drawing you back.

See the word in context

He discovered an unexpected affinity for pottery the very first time he touched a spinning wheel.

A child who has never had a formal lesson sits down at a piano and shows an instant affinity for it, picking out simple tunes by ear, grinning the entire time, and asking to play again the next day.

Her liking for the instrument is natural and easy — nobody taught her to feel it. Unlike 'talent,' which describes skill, affinity describes the pull of genuine liking itself.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    fondness: Fondness is a warm liking that grows out of long familiarity, for a favorite sweater, an old song, a place you know well. An affinity does not need time: it is a natural pull you can feel the very first time you meet something.

    attraction: An attraction is any pull toward something, and it can be brief: a bright window display attracts you for a moment. An affinity is a lasting natural fit between you and a subject or activity, a pull that keeps drawing you back.

    inclination: An inclination is a leaning toward doing or choosing something, like an inclination to sleep late. It describes a pattern in behavior, not a feeling. An affinity is the felt liking itself, a warm natural pull toward the thing.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    aversion: a strong dislike or unwillingness

    distaste: a dislike for something

    Where it sits · how strong the pull toward it is

    interest → affinity → passion

    interest vs. affinity: Interest is weaker than affinity — you can be interested in something without feeling truly drawn to it, while affinity is a genuine natural pull.

    passion vs. affinity: Passion is stronger than affinity: affinity is a natural liking, but passion is an intense, driving devotion that goes far beyond simple liking.
