What It Means

Homogeneous means all of the same kind throughout, part after part after part.

See the word in context

The choir sounded almost homogeneous, forty voices blending into what seemed like a single one.

On field-trip day the cafeteria line is perfectly homogeneous: every single student receives the identical boxed lunch -- the same sandwich, the same apple, the same carton of juice, five hundred times over.

Every unit is the same kind as every other, with no variety anywhere. Homogeneous describes wholes whose parts are all of the same kind, from crowds to mixtures to rows of matching houses.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    unvaried: Unvaried complains about a run of sameness over a stretch of time -- an unvaried diet, an unvaried routine, no change from one week to the next. Homogeneous describes a group's makeup at one moment: members all of one kind throughout. A cafeteria menu can be unvaried; a committee whose members are all of one kind is homogeneous.

    matching: Matching says items were picked or made to go together -- matching socks, matching mugs on a shelf. Homogeneous needs nobody's choosing: it describes a group that simply is the same kind throughout. A place setting is matching; a crowd all of one kind is homogeneous.

    consistent: Consistent tracks one thing across time and praises its sameness from one occasion to the next -- a consistent batting stance, consistent grades. Homogeneous looks across a whole group at a single moment and finds every member the same kind. A batter is consistent; a neighborhood can be homogeneous.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    varied: of many different types

    mixed: made up of different sorts

    Where it sits · how uniform the group is

    alike → homogeneous → identical

    alike vs. homogeneous: Alike is looser and milder than homogeneous -- things that are alike share noticeable features, while a homogeneous group is the same kind through and through, part after part after part.

    identical vs. homogeneous: Identical goes beyond even homogeneous: homogeneous members all belong to one kind, while identical ones cannot be told apart at all -- not one distinguishing detail between them.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Prefix check every time: Homo- means same, hetero- means different. In science, a homogeneous mixture like salt water looks the same in every drop, while a heterogeneous one like trail mix keeps its different pieces visible. Outside the lab, homogeneous often carries a hint of dullness -- a homogeneous neighborhood or playlist could use some variety.
