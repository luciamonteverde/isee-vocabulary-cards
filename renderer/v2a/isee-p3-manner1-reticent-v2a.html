What It Means

Reticent is reluctant to speak freely or share one's thoughts and feelings.

See the word in context

Normally reticent about her personal life, Elena surprised everyone by sharing the whole story at dinner.

When asked how the surgery went, the usually reticent surgeon just nodded once and walked away, offering nothing more.

Reticent isn't about being unable to talk -- it's about choosing to hold back, often on purpose.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    reserved: Reserved describes a whole temperament: a reserved person is restrained and self-contained in general, revealing little of themselves in any setting. Reticent is narrower and more deliberate -- it often attaches to a single subject a person chooses to keep back, whatever they are like the rest of the time.

    guarded: Guarded means cautious about what you reveal because you sense a risk -- a guarded answer is worded carefully so it cannot be used against you. Reticent is quieter and less defensive: a reticent person simply declines to volunteer things, with no sense of fending off a threat.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    unreserved: willing to speak openly, without holding back

    forthcoming: willing to share information freely

    Where it sits · how deliberately words are held back

    quiet → reticent → secretive

    quiet vs. reticent: Quiet just means not talking much, and implies no reason for it; reticent goes further -- a reticent person is deliberately holding their own thoughts and feelings back.

    secretive vs. reticent: Secretive pushes reticence further into actively concealing information, not just declining to volunteer it.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Reticent describes someone who holds back thoughts and feelings rather than volunteering them. This unit's terse (blunt and brief once someone does speak) and succinct (clear and efficient, a compliment) both describe how something is said, not whether the speaker wants to say it at all -- a reticent person might avoid the topic altogether.
