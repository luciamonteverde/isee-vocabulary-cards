enigma

ih-NIG-muh -- from a Greek word meaning a riddle.

What It Means

An enigma is a person or thing so puzzling that it resists explanation no matter how closely people study it.

See the word in context

The scientist's sudden disappearance remained an enigma that investigators never solved.

A locked diary written in a code no one has ever cracked sits in a museum display case, an enigma that leaves every visitor with more questions than answers.

That puzzling, unexplained quality is exactly what makes something an enigma. Unlike 'fact,' which is clearly known and proven, an enigma resists explanation.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    riddle: A riddle is a puzzling question deliberately posed as a game or challenge, built to be solved by a clever answer. An enigma is the opposite kind of puzzle: it is not designed by anyone and offers no answer key, staying baffling however long people study it.

    paradox: A paradox is a statement or situation that seems to contradict itself -- two things that cannot both be true, yet somehow are. An enigma need not contain any contradiction; it is simply a person or thing that resists explanation, no matter how closely people look.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    certainty: something that is clearly known or established as true

    fact: something known to be true

    Where it sits · how much it resists explanation

    puzzle → enigma → mystery

    puzzle vs. enigma: Puzzle is milder than enigma -- a puzzle is often solvable with enough effort, while an enigma suggests something that resists explanation altogether.

    mystery vs. enigma: Mystery can feel even deeper than enigma: enigma often describes a specific puzzling detail, while mystery can describe an entire unexplained situation, often with an eerie or dramatic quality.
