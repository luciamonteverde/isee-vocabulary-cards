What It Means

Ebullient means cheerful and so full of energy that it visibly overflows, as an ongoing trait rather than a one-time burst of excitement.

See the word in context

Even after losing the match, the ebullient coach kept cracking jokes at practice.

The ebullient birthday girl couldn't stop bouncing around the room greeting every guest.

Ebullient describes an ongoing personality trait -- a general, overflowing cheerfulness that shows up again and again, not just once.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    bubbly: A bubbly person is lively and cheerful in an easy, everyday way -- the classmate who chats brightly with everyone. Ebullient is the stronger, more formal word: cheerfulness and energy boiling over so visibly that it fills the whole room. Bubbly fizzes pleasantly; ebullient overflows.

    buoyant: Buoyant means cheerful in a way that stays afloat -- a buoyant mood keeps riding above bad news and setbacks without sinking. Ebullient is louder and more physical: the good spirits do not just stay up, they spill out in bouncing, joking, unstoppable energy for everyone to see.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    gloomy: showing sadness or low spirits

    glum: looking or feeling dejected

    Where it sits · how intense the joy becomes

    cheerful → ebullient → jubilant

    cheerful vs. ebullient: Being cheerful is a mild, everyday pleasantness; being ebullient means overflowing with visible energy.

    jubilant vs. ebullient: Jubilant describes triumphant joy tied to a specific victory, a sharper peak than everyday ebullient cheer.
