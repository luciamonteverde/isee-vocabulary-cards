automatic

aw-tuh-MAT-ik

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Automatic describes something that happens or gets done by itself, with nobody having to decide it, think about it, or work it each time — a machine that runs on its own once it is switched on, or an action a person does the same way every time without a single thought.

See the word in context

Ravi never decided to duck when the ball flew toward the bench; the movement was automatic, finished before he even knew the ball was coming.

The light over the front door is automatic: a small device turns it on the moment the sky gets dark, and nobody in the family has touched that switch since it was installed.

Nobody in the house decides each evening that the light should come on, and nobody turns it on. The device does the whole job by itself, the same way every night, which is exactly the test for automatic: no person has to decide or act. Notice that the light is not merely quick, and it is not on a schedule a person follows — it simply happens on its own. If a family member walked over and turned the switch on every evening as it got dark, however regularly, the light would not be automatic.

    Accuracy

    “Does this word fit the meaning exactly, or does a neighbour fit better?”

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    mechanical: Mechanical describes the way an action looks or sounds — cold and empty, like a machine going through the same steps over and over, with no feeling in it. It is a judgment about manner, and it is usually a complaint. Automatic is about deciding, not manner: nothing had to be chosen or thought about, and the action can still be warm and friendly. Ask whether the sentence is complaining that the action looked cold and empty, or saying that it happened without anyone deciding it.

    immediate: Immediate means happening right away, with no delay at all — and it says nothing about whether anyone chose it. A doctor's immediate response to an emergency can be a careful decision made fast. Automatic says no decision happened at all. Ask whether the sentence is about how quickly something came or about whether anyone had to decide it.

    spontaneous: Spontaneous describes something you do right then and there, from your own sudden urge — nobody planned it, but you did choose it, in that moment, and it felt alive. Automatic has no urge and no choice in it: it happens the same way every time, whether you feel like it or not. Ask whether the person felt a sudden wish and acted on it, or whether the thing simply happened on its own.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    deliberate: chosen on purpose and thought through with care before it is done.

    manual: worked by a person's own hands, step by step, each time.

    Where it sits · how much a person has to think about doing something

    routine → automatic → unconscious

    routine vs. automatic: Routine is the mild end — something done regularly, as part of the normal pattern of a day or a job, like a routine check-up or a routine inspection. It happens on a schedule, but a person still does it on purpose, with attention, every time. Automatic needs no attention at all. Ask whether the sentence is about something that happens regularly because someone does it, or about something that happens without anyone deciding.

    unconscious vs. automatic: Unconscious is the far end — the person does not even know they are doing it, like shaking a foot under the desk or playing with their hair, and would be surprised to be told. An automatic action is done without deciding, but you still know it is happening: you know you locked the door, you just never had to think about it. Ask whether the sentence says the person did not know about the action at all, or only that they did it without having to think.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Automatic works for machines and for people alike: an automatic door, an automatic payment, an automatic reply, an automatic smile. With people it does not say the action was cold — a warm 'thank you' can be automatic if it comes out without a thought — so it is not an insult the way a word like mechanical is. You will also see the form automatically ('the file saves automatically'), and you will meet automatic meaning 'guaranteed to follow': an automatic win, an automatic penalty — the same idea, since nobody has to decide it. On the ISEE it appears in sentence completion, usually for a reaction that happens before a person has time to think.
