What It Means

Servile means excessively willing to serve, obey, and please, especially the powerful -- a self-lowering eagerness that gives up self-respect.

See the word in context

The new waiter's servile flattery -- constant compliments and low bows -- made the other diners visibly uncomfortable.

Employees noticed that the assistant manager grew strangely servile whenever the regional director walked in, laughing at every joke and fetching coffee unasked.

The sudden, excessive eagerness to please someone more powerful -- laughing at every joke, fetching coffee unasked -- is exactly the servile behavior the word describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    fawning: Fawning is showy, gushing flattery -- compliments, admiring looks, exaggerated delight aimed at charming someone. Servile behavior may involve no flattery at all: it is an excessive, self-lowering readiness to serve and obey, especially toward the powerful -- fetching, agreeing, hovering.

    groveling: Groveling is open self-abasement -- begging, pleading, flattening yourself before someone in fear or desperation. Servile behavior is quieter and more habitual: an over-eager willingness to serve and please that gives up self-respect without the begging.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    defiant: boldly resistant to authority

    independent: free from outside control or influence

    Where it sits · how far eagerness to please goes

    helpful → servile → obsequious

    helpful vs. servile: Being helpful just means willing to assist; being servile means being excessively, almost uncomfortably eager to please, at the cost of self-respect.

    obsequious vs. servile: Obsequious pushes servile even further, adding a note of insincere, calculated flattery aimed specifically at gaining favor.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Servile describes a personality trait or manner directed at pleasing people, especially those in power. Compare "slavish" (this same unit), which describes blind imitation of ideas or rules rather than eagerness to please a person.
