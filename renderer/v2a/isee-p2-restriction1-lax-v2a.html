Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Lax means not strict or careful where firmness is required, a settled slackness in rules, standards, or enforcement.

See the word in context

Years of lax enforcement let drivers treat the school zone's speed limit as a polite suggestion.

Security at the gate is lax: the night guard waves delivery trucks through without checking a single ID, and half the cameras have been broken for months.

That loose, careless setup is nowhere near strict or careful enough for the job it is supposed to do. Lax describes rules, standards, or people that have gone slack exactly where firmness is required.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    careless: Careless means not giving enough attention to whatever you are doing -- typos, spills, a form filled out wrong. Lax is narrower and aims at rules: lax describes standards, security, or enforcement gone soft where strictness is required. A careless person slips; a lax system stops demanding.

    permissive: Permissive describes an intentional choice to allow things -- a permissive parent decides not to forbid much. Lax is not a choice but a failure: the rule still officially demands strictness, and lax enforcement simply stops delivering it. Permissive loosens the rules on purpose; lax lets them sag.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    rigorous: extremely thorough, careful, and strict

    strict: demanding that rules be followed exactly

    Where it sits · how blameworthy the looseness is

    slack → lax → negligent

    slack vs. lax: Slack is looser and more casual than lax -- effort can go slack for an afternoon, while lax describes a settled failure of strictness where real care is required, like lax security or lax grading.

    negligent vs. lax: Negligent is the harsher judgment: lax standards are merely too loose, while negligence crosses into blameworthy failure -- the kind of carelessness that courts and inspectors hold people responsible for.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Lax pairs naturally with enforcement, security, standards, discipline, and supervision: 'lax security,' 'lax about deadlines.' One writing trap: lax ('not strict') sounds exactly like 'lacks' ('does not have') -- spelling and context have to keep the two apart.
