Hear It

What It Means

Restive is impatient and stubborn; restless and resistant to being controlled.

See the word in context

Union members grew restive during the fourth straight week without a new contract offer from management.

After sitting on the tarmac for two hours with no explanation, the restive passengers began pressing the call buttons and demanding answers from the flight crew.

Growing impatient and pushing back against being kept waiting without explanation is exactly the kind of restless resistance restive describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    restless: Restless means unable to stay still or settle -- a restless sleeper tosses and turns with nothing actually holding him down. Restive is narrower: restlessness that pushes back against being controlled or made to wait, the way a restive horse strains at its reins.

    defiant: Defiant means openly refusing to obey -- a defiant student says no to the rule out loud. Restive is less direct: a restive crowd has not refused anything yet; it shifts, mutters, and strains against being held, on the edge of disobedience rather than past it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    docile: easily managed or controlled

    calm: free from agitation or excitement

    Where it sits · how strongly it strains against control

    impatient → restive → unruly

    impatient vs. restive: Impatient is milder than restive: an impatient customer sighs and checks the time, while a restive one has moved from waiting to resisting -- pressing forward, questioning the guards, straining against being held back.

    unruly vs. restive: Unruly is stronger than restive: a restive crowd shifts and grumbles against being made to wait, while an unruly one breaks loose entirely, pushing past the barriers.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Restive describes active, visible resistance to being controlled or held back -- a restive crowd or animal is straining against restraint, which is stronger than simply feeling bored or unhappy.
