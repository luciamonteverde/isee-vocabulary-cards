penultimate

pih-NUL-tuh-mit -- from Latin paene ('almost') + ultimus ('last').

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Penultimate is second to last in a series of things.

See the word in context

The penultimate chapter reveals the killer's identity, saving the final confrontation for last.

In the penultimate episode of the season, the two rival teams finally agree to work together, setting up next week's series finale.

Notice that this isn't the finale itself -- it's the episode right before it, the one that sets up the true final moment still to come. That specific 'one before the very last' position is what penultimate always points to.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    second-to-last: Second-to-last says in plain, everyday words exactly what penultimate means: the one just before the final item. Penultimate is the formal single-word version you meet in books and test questions, while second-to-last is how you would say it aloud to a friend.

    next-to-final: Next-to-final describes the same position as penultimate -- coming immediately before the last item in a series -- but it is a built-on-the-spot phrase rather than a dictionary word. Writers choose penultimate when they want one precise, polished term for that slot.

    antepenultimate: Antepenultimate counts one step further back: third from the end, the item right before the penultimate one. Both words number a series from the finish line, but antepenultimate stops one slot earlier than penultimate does.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    first: coming before all the others in a series

    initial: occurring at the very beginning

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Penultimate refers to a specific position -- second to last -- not just 'near the end' in a loose sense. It's often mistaken for meaning something extra-important or 'ultimate,' but it actually means the opposite of last.
