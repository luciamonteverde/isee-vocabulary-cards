obstruct

ub-STRUKT -- Latin ob- ('in the way') plus struere ('to build, pile up'): to build something in the way.

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

To obstruct is to block movement or progress outright, closing the way off entirely rather than merely slowing it.

See the word in context

A jackknifed truck managed to obstruct all three lanes of the highway for hours.

Overnight, a rockslide dumps a wall of boulders that obstructs the only road into the valley, and no vehicle can get in or out until crews clear it.

That complete blocking of the way is the difference: impede merely slows progress down, while obstruct means the path is closed off entirely.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    clog: To clog is to block a passage gradually as things pile up inside it -- grease clogs a drain, hair clogs a shower. Obstruct covers any blocking of a way, sudden or slow, while clog pictures a channel slowly filling from within.

    bar: To bar is to shut a way against someone on purpose -- a bolted door, a guard barring entry. To obstruct is simply to be in the way: a fallen tree obstructs a road without anyone intending anything.

    choke: To choke a passage is to narrow it until almost nothing squeezes through -- leaves choke a gutter, weeds choke a stream. Obstruct is the blunter claim: the way is blocked. A choked pipe still dribbles; an obstructed one is shut.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    clear: to remove whatever blocks a path

    unblock: to open up a blocked passage

    Where it sits · how deliberately the path is closed

    block → obstruct → barricade

    block vs. obstruct: Block is the plain, everyday version of obstruct -- anything can block a path for a moment, while obstruct sounds more serious and often implies deliberate or damaging blockage.

    barricade vs. obstruct: Barricade is more dramatic than obstruct: to obstruct is to block a way by any means, while to barricade is to build a deliberate barrier, often for defense.
