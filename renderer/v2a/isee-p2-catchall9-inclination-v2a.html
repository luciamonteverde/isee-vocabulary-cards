What It Means

Inclination is a natural pull toward acting or feeling in a particular way, softer and less settled than a firm preference.

See the word in context

She had a natural inclination toward solving puzzles rather than reading fiction.

Given a free afternoon, one student heads straight for the chess club while her twin heads for the art studio, each following her own inclination without ever having to think twice about it.

That built-in pull toward one activity over another is exactly what inclination means. Unlike 'obligation,' which is a duty imposed from outside, inclination comes from a person's own nature.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    predisposition: A predisposition is a built-in tendency you start out with, often from birth or temperament -- a predisposition to allergies, or to worry. An inclination is the felt leaning itself, the pull you notice toward one choice, and it can form or fade far more easily.

    preference: A preference is a settled choice between options: you know you like tea over coffee. An inclination is softer and less decided -- a natural leaning in one direction that you may not have examined and might never act on.

    leaning: A leaning is a mild tilt toward one side that shows up over time -- artistic leanings, political leanings -- usually about broad tastes. An inclination is the pull itself in a particular matter: the leaning you feel toward one action or choice, whether or not you ever examine it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    aversion: a strong dislike or unwillingness toward something

    reluctance: unwillingness to do something

    Where it sits · how strong and personal the leaning is

    tendency → inclination → penchant

    tendency vs. inclination: Tendency is a more everyday, neutral word than inclination -- tendency can describe any habitual pattern, while inclination specifically implies a personal, felt leaning toward one choice.

    penchant vs. inclination: Penchant can feel stronger than inclination: inclination is a general leaning toward something, while penchant implies a fondness so strong it borders on a passion.
