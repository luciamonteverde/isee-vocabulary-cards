What It Means

Ruddy is having a lasting, healthy reddish color, especially in the face, deeper and stronger than a soft pink tint.

See the word in context

The sculptor spent hours mixing paint to capture the innkeeper's ruddy, weathered complexion.

After a lifetime of working outdoors, his ruddy cheeks and nose made him look like he'd stepped out of a holiday card.

Ruddy specifically describes a lasting, healthy reddish tone in the skin, the settled color of a face shaped by weather, work, or hearty health.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    rosy: Rosy is a soft, healthy pink — the delicate color of a child's cheeks or a light morning glow. Ruddy is deeper and redder: the settled, robust color of a face shaped by weather, work, or hearty health. Rosy suggests a gentle tint, while ruddy suggests a strong, weathered red.

    flushed: Flushed means reddened suddenly and briefly — by heat, exertion, or a rush of emotion — and the color fades once the moment passes. Ruddy is the opposite in one way: a lasting, characteristic redness that belongs to the face itself. A sprint leaves you flushed; a fisherman's face stays ruddy year-round.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    pale: having little color in the face; light-toned

    pallid: unhealthily pale

    Where it sits · how strong the facial color is

    pinkish → ruddy → flaming red

    pinkish vs. ruddy: Pinkish means having only a faint tinge of pink, a milder color than ruddy.

    flaming red vs. ruddy: Flaming red describes an intensely bright red, well beyond the healthy glow of ruddy.
