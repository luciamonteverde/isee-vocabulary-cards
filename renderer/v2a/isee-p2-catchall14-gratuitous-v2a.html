gratuitous

gruh-TOO-uh-tus -- from Latin gratuitus, 'free, given as a favor,' from gratia, 'favor, kindness': something gratuitous is given without being asked for.

Meaning

What It Means

Gratuitous means uncalled for: added deliberately and without real justification, often just to make a point or for effect.

See the word in context

The critic complained that the play's extra twenty minutes of unrelated dance numbers felt entirely gratuitous.

The coach added a gratuitous, unannounced extra lap around the field purely to make an example of the one player who had arrived two minutes late.

Gratuitous describes something added without real justification, often just to make a point or for effect -- an extra lap that punishes the whole team for one late arrival serves no real training purpose beyond making an example.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    excessive: Excessive means too much of something that may be perfectly fine in smaller amounts -- salt, homework, screen time. Gratuitous attacks the justification instead of the quantity: even one gratuitous insult is one too many, because nothing in the situation called for it at all.

    superfluous: Superfluous describes excess that is only in the way -- a tenth pencil, a second umbrella -- left over beyond what is needed, with no blame attached. Gratuitous is an accusation: the extra thing was added deliberately and without justification, often to shock or wound. A superfluous scene pads a film; a gratuitous one offends.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    justified: shown to be right or reasonable

    necessary: required; needed

    Where it sits · how little justification it has

    needless → gratuitous → unwarranted

    needless vs. gratuitous: Needless is a plain, everyday word for anything unnecessary, while gratuitous adds a sharper criticism that something was added deliberately and inappropriately.

    unwarranted vs. gratuitous: Unwarranted goes a step further than gratuitous by emphasizing that something wasn't just unnecessary but actively lacked any real justification.
