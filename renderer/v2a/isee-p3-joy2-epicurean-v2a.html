What It Means

Epicurean is devoted to the pursuit of refined, sensory pleasure.

See the word in context

The couple's epicurean honeymoon consisted of nothing but tasting menus and rare vintages.

Their epicurean weekend consisted of a five-course tasting menu and a private wine cellar tour.

Epicurean describes a lifestyle devoted to refined pleasure, especially good food, wine, and comfort.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    extravagant: Extravagant is about cost: spending or using far more money or resources than necessary. Epicurean says nothing about price — it means devoted to refined, sensory pleasure, especially food and drink. A five-dollar bowl of perfect noodles can be an epicurean joy; a gold-plated water bottle is merely extravagant.

    luxurious: Luxurious describes things that surround you with comfort and expense — a plush suite, a silk robe. Epicurean describes a person or way of life actively devoted to seeking out refined pleasure, especially in taste. A hotel room can be luxurious; only the guest chasing the city's best table is epicurean.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    modest: unpretentious in scale or amount

    spartan: showing strict self-discipline and avoidance of comfort

    Where it sits · how central pleasure is to life

    pampered → epicurean → excessive in every comfort

    pampered vs. epicurean: Being pampered is enjoying comfort provided by others; an epicurean actively seeks out refined pleasure as a way of life.

    excessive in every comfort vs. epicurean: Taking comfort to an unhealthy extreme goes even further than simply living an epicurean lifestyle.
