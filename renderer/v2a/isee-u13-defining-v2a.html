Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Defining describes the feature, quality, or moment that tells you what something or someone is — the one thing you would name to say what makes it itself, so that without it the thing would no longer be the same thing at all.

See the word in context

The defining feature of the old lighthouse is its tower painted in red and white rings — paint it plain gray and sailors would no longer know which coast they were passing.

For the tiny book shop, the defining moment came the night of the blackout, when the owner lit candles, kept the doors open, and half the street came in to read by the glow.

The blackout night is defining because it fixed what the shop IS in everyone's mind — the place that stays open for its neighbors. It was not a busy night or a big sale; it is the moment people would name afterward to explain what kind of shop this is. A defining moment shows or fixes what someone or something is, not merely something important that happened.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    distinctive: Distinctive means easy to notice and different from the rest — a distinctive laugh, a distinctive hat — but the noticeable detail can be a small one that changes nothing about what the thing is. Defining goes to the heart: it is the feature you would use to say what the thing IS. Ask whether removing the feature would only make the thing blend in, or would make it something else entirely.

    crucial: Crucial means a great deal depends on it — the make-or-break point in one particular situation, where the outcome is won or lost. Defining is about identity rather than outcome: a defining moment shows or fixes who someone is, whether or not anything was won. Ask whether the sentence is about what hangs on the moment, or about what the moment reveals.

    essential: Essential means a thing cannot work or exist without it — an engine is essential to a car — but every car has one, so it says nothing about which car this is. Defining is the feature that tells this thing apart and makes it itself. Ask whether the feature is needed to make the thing work, or needed to tell what the thing is.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    minor: small and easy to leave out

    trivial: too small to tell you anything about the thing

    Where it sits · how much a feature tells you about what something is

    typical → characteristic → defining

    typical vs. defining: Typical is the weakest — a typical feature is one most members of the group share, so it tells you almost nothing about which one you are looking at: four wheels are typical of a car. Defining picks out the thing itself. Ask whether most others of its kind have the feature too.

    characteristic vs. defining: Characteristic is closer — a characteristic feature belongs to this thing in particular and helps you recognize it, but it is one mark among several. Defining is the single feature you would name to say what the thing is, the one it could not lose and still be itself. Ask whether it is one clue among several or the whole identity.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Defining is almost always paired with a noun such as moment, feature, quality, or trait: a defining moment, the defining feature. On the ISEE it tends to appear in the sentences with a blank, about a career, an animal, or a style, where the clue is that one thing shows what the whole is. Do not mix it up with the verb define — a defining feature does the defining; it marks the thing out as itself.
