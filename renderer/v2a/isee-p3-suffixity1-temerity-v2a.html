temerity

tuh-MER-i-tee

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Temerity is excessive boldness -- daring that oversteps and borders on rude, foolish, or reckless.

See the word in context

It took a certain temerity for the substitute teacher to rearrange the seating chart on her very first day.

Reporters were stunned by the intern's temerity when she stood up and asked the CEO, in front of the entire press corps, why he had laid off a thousand workers the same week he received a bonus.

Temerity always implies a boldness that oversteps -- it's daring that borders on rude, foolish, or reckless.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    audacity: Audacity is shocking boldness, and it can cut either way -- a daring plan can be admired for its audacity. Temerity never earns admiration: it is boldness that has overstepped into rashness, and the word always carries disapproval.

    impudence: Impudence is bold disrespect aimed at a person -- cheeky, rude behavior toward someone who expects better manners. Temerity is rash overstepping of any kind: an act can show temerity without insulting anyone at all.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    timidity: a lack of courage or confidence

    caution: careful attention to avoiding risk

    Where it sits · how far boldness outruns caution

    confidence → temerity → recklessness

    confidence vs. temerity: Confidence is simply a healthy trust in one's own abilities, without the reckless overstepping of temerity.

    recklessness vs. temerity: Recklessness is an even more extreme, careless disregard for danger or consequences than ordinary temerity.

    Word Choice
