psyche

SY-kee -- the same Greek root that gives us 'psychology.'

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Psyche is a person's whole inner mental and emotional life, taking in thoughts, emotions, fears, and memories, not just the thinking mind.

See the word in context

Years of intense competition had left a lasting mark on the athlete's psyche.

A soldier returns home physically unharmed, yet his psyche carries what his body escaped: he finds himself unable to sleep through the night, startled by ordinary sounds that never used to bother him before deployment.

The deep, often invisible part of him that carries that mark -- his mind and spirit -- is his psyche. Unlike 'body,' which is physical, psyche is entirely about the inner self.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    inner self: Your inner self is your true, private nature -- who you are when no one is watching. Psyche is the wider psychological term: the whole mind and its workings, including fears, memories, and emotions, healthy or wounded.

    intellect: The intellect is the reasoning, problem-solving part of the mind. The psyche is far broader: it takes in emotions, fears, memories, and instincts -- the whole inner life, not just the thinking part.

    conscience: The conscience is the inner voice that judges right from wrong. The psyche contains it and much more -- a person's entire mental and emotional world, of which moral judgment is only one corner.

    temperament: Temperament is a person's built-in emotional style -- whether they run calm, anxious, or fiery by nature. The psyche is the whole inner territory; temperament is more like its prevailing weather.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    body: the physical structure of a person

    physique: a person's physical build or muscular structure

    Where it sits · how deep the inner life goes

    mind → psyche → spirit

    mind vs. psyche: Mind is a more everyday word than psyche -- mind often refers just to everyday thoughts, while psyche is a more formal, psychological term for someone's entire inner mental and emotional life.

    spirit vs. psyche: Spirit can feel even deeper than psyche in some contexts: psyche often describes the mind's psychological workings, while spirit can suggest something more essential or even soulful about a person.
