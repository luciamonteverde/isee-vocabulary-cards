What It Means

Deadpan is deliberately showing no emotion or expression, especially for comic effect.

See the word in context

The news anchor read the day's most ridiculous headlines with the exact same deadpan delivery he used for serious news.

The poker player's deadpan face gave away nothing during the final hand, even though he was holding the best cards of the night.

The player wasn't hiding a secret weapon -- he was simply keeping his expression blank on purpose, exactly what deadpan describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    expressionless: An expressionless face simply shows no feeling, for any reason at all -- boredom, fatigue, or nothing behind it. Deadpan adds intent: the blankness is deliberate, usually staged so something absurd lands as funny. Expressionless describes the look; deadpan describes the performance.

    impassive: Impassive means genuinely unmoved -- the person feels no swell of emotion, or refuses to be affected. Deadpan is an act: the speaker is often amused underneath and keeps a flat face on purpose for comic effect. Impassive is a real state; deadpan is a performance.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    animated: full of life, energy, and expression

    expressive: clearly showing feeling or emotion

    Where it sits · how blank the face stays

    reserved → deadpan → wooden

    reserved vs. deadpan: Someone reserved is quiet and holds back emotion somewhat, milder than fully deadpan.

    wooden vs. deadpan: A wooden expression is stiff and completely lifeless, an even more extreme blankness than deadpan.
