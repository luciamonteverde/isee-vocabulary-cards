philanthropy

fih-LAN-thruh-pee -- built from Greek roots meaning 'love' and 'humankind.'

What It Means

Philanthropy is promoting the welfare of others through an organized, sustained effort of giving money or resources away on a large scale.

See the word in context

Her decades of philanthropy funded schools, hospitals, and libraries across the region.

In an act of quiet philanthropy, a retired businessman donates most of his fortune to build clean water wells in villages he will likely never visit himself.

That generous goodwill directed toward helping humanity, often through giving, is exactly what philanthropy describes. Unlike 'business,' which seeks profit, philanthropy seeks to help others without expecting anything back.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    generosity: Generosity is the willingness to give freely -- of money, time, or effort, in any amount. Philanthropy channels that generosity into something bigger: an organized, sustained effort to improve people's lives, usually through large gifts of money.

    benevolence: Benevolence is a kindly, charitable disposition -- the standing wish to do others good, whether or not any money is involved. Philanthropy is the concrete practice built on such goodwill: actually giving money and resources away, on an organized scale.

    altruism: Altruism is the selfless motive itself -- caring about other people's well-being with no thought of reward, shown in any unselfish act, large or small. Philanthropy is narrower and more concrete: the organized practice of giving money or resources away at scale.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    selfishness: a lack of concern for others, focused only on oneself

    greed: an intense desire for wealth beyond what one needs

    Where it sits · how large and organized the giving is

    charity → philanthropy → endowment

    endowment vs. philanthropy: An endowment is even more organized and lasting than philanthropy: philanthropy is the ongoing practice of giving generously, while an endowment locks that giving into a permanent fund designed to keep paying out forever.

    charity vs. philanthropy: Charity is a more everyday word than philanthropy -- charity can describe any act of giving, large or small, while philanthropy often implies a larger, more organized, and sustained effort to help humanity.
