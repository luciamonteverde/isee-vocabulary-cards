What It Means

Alacrity means brisk, cheerful speed in responding, eagerness showing up as immediate action the moment the call comes.

See the word in context

Told that pizza was on its way, the entire study group packed up their books with unusual alacrity.

The moment the fire alarm sounded, the trained staff evacuated the building with alacrity, guiding visitors calmly but quickly toward the exits.

Alacrity combines speed with eagerness -- it's not just doing something fast, but doing it fast because you actually want to.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    eagerness: Eagerness is the feeling of really wanting to do something -- the excitement itself. Alacrity is that excitement showing up as action: a brisk, immediate, cheerful response. You can sit quietly full of eagerness for an hour; alacrity exists only in the moment you spring up and move.

    readiness: Readiness is the prepared state before anything happens -- bags packed, plans made, willing to act. Alacrity is the lively speed of the response itself once the call actually comes. A crew can wait in readiness all night; alacrity appears the instant they leap to work.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    reluctance: unwillingness or hesitation to do something

    sluggishness: a slow, lazy lack of energy

    Where it sits · how eagerly you jump in

    willingness → alacrity → zeal

    willingness vs. alacrity: Willingness is simply being okay with doing something, without the brisk energy of alacrity.

    zeal vs. alacrity: Zeal is an even more intense, passionate energy than ordinary alacrity, often lasting well beyond a single moment.
