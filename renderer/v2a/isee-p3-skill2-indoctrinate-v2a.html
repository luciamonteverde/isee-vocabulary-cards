What It Means

To indoctrinate is to teach someone to accept beliefs without allowing them to question those beliefs.

See the word in context

The group was accused of trying to indoctrinate new members before they had a chance to question its teachings.

The training video was designed to indoctrinate recruits so thoroughly that questioning company policy would feel unthinkable.

Indoctrinate carries a slightly negative charge -- it's not just teaching, but implanting belief in a way that discourages independent thought or doubt.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    drill: To drill something into someone is to fix it in memory through sheer repetition -- spelling rules, safety steps, multiplication tables. Drilling targets facts and habits and leaves the learner free to think; to indoctrinate goes after beliefs themselves, teaching them so one-sidedly that questioning feels off-limits.

    condition: To condition someone is to train an automatic response into them -- praise here, punishment there -- until the reaction fires without thought. Conditioning shapes behavior; to indoctrinate targets belief, teaching a doctrine as unquestionable truth rather than installing a reflex.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    liberate: free someone from an imposed belief

    question: encourage doubt and independent inquiry

    Where it sits · how totally beliefs get taken over

    influence → indoctrinate → brainwash

    influence vs. indoctrinate: You can influence someone's opinion with a single good argument, and they stay free to disagree; to indoctrinate goes much further, planting a whole set of beliefs and closing the door on questions.

    brainwash vs. indoctrinate: To brainwash someone is a far more total, forceful process than a single influence, well beyond ordinary indoctrination.

    Word Choice

    Indoctrinate is a formal, somewhat clinical word with a critical edge -- it almost always implies the teaching was one-sided or manipulative, unlike plain words such as 'teach' or 'train.'
