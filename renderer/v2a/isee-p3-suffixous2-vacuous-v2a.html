What It Means

Vacuous means empty of any real thought or intelligence, with no serious content at all.

See the word in context

The talk show host's vacuous chatter filled the airtime without ever saying anything memorable.

The motivational poster's vacuous slogan -- 'Dream Big, Achieve More' -- offered no actual advice for anyone struggling to reach their goals.

The words sound inspiring but contain no real substance or thought, which is exactly what makes a phrase vacuous.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    vapid: Vapid describes something flat, stale, and lifeless -- small talk about nothing, a bland show, soda gone flat. It fails to interest or stimulate. Vacuous is a harsher charge: not merely dull, but empty of any thought or intelligence at all.

    inane: Inane means silly to the point of absurdity -- an inane joke, an inane question that no one needed to ask. It mocks a thing for being ridiculous. Vacuous is colder: a vacuous remark is not laughably silly, just hollow, with no mind behind it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    insightful: having or showing an accurate, deep understanding

    substantive: having real, meaningful content

    Where it sits · how empty of thought it is

    shallow → vacuous → mindless

    shallow vs. vacuous: Shallow is milder than vacuous: a shallow remark lacks depth, but a vacuous one is emptier still, showing almost no thought or serious content at all.

    mindless vs. vacuous: Mindless is harsher than vacuous: a vacuous comment is empty of real thought, while mindless goes further, suggesting no thinking is happening at all.
