proliferate

proh-LIF-uh-rayt

Section 1

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

To proliferate is to increase rapidly in number.

See the word in context

Online rumors about the store's closure proliferated within hours of the first post.

Cheap knockoff versions of the toy proliferated across marketplaces almost as soon as the original went viral.

This wasn't a slow trickle -- countless copies appeared everywhere almost at once, which is exactly proliferate.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    multiply: To multiply is to increase greatly in number, at whatever pace — a town's shops can multiply over fifty years. To proliferate is to multiply fast: new copies appearing everywhere almost at once, the way rumors or knockoffs do.

    spread: To spread is to reach more people or places — a story, a habit, or a fashion spreads without anything new being made. For things to proliferate, they must actually grow in number, and quickly; spreading is about territory, not count.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    diminish: become or make smaller in number or amount

    Where it sits · how fast the numbers multiply

    increase → proliferate → explode

    increase vs. proliferate: To increase is simply to grow larger in amount or number, at any pace; to proliferate is specifically to shoot up rapidly in number.

    explode vs. proliferate: Proliferate describes rapid, sustained multiplication; explode goes further — the numbers burst upward all at once, in one sudden, dramatic jump.
