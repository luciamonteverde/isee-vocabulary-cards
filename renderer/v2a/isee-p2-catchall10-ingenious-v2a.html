ingenious

in-JEEN-yus -- not to be confused with 'ingenuous,' meaning innocent or naive.

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Ingenious means strikingly original and clever in a way that actually works, solving a specific problem in an unexpected way.

See the word in context

The engineers devised an ingenious solution that used half the material at twice the strength.

A student with no money for a science fair budget builds an ingenious working water filter entirely out of a plastic bottle, sand, and charcoal, and it outperforms every store-bought model in the competition.

That clever, original problem-solving is exactly what ingenious describes. Unlike 'expensive,' which describes cost, ingenious describes clever, resourceful thinking.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    inventive: Inventive praises a person's ongoing ability to keep creating new things and ideas. Ingenious usually praises one specific piece of thinking -- an ingenious fix, an ingenious design -- brilliant for how cleverly it solves the problem in front of it.

    resourceful: Resourceful means good at making do with whatever happens to be on hand. Ingenious goes further: an ingenious solution is strikingly original, something nobody had thought of before, while a resourceful one may simply use ordinary things sensibly under pressure.

    imaginative: Imaginative means full of new ideas and vivid mental pictures, whether or not they solve anything. Ingenious adds usefulness to the originality: an ingenious idea is not just imagined, it cleverly works, solving a real problem in an unexpected way.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    unimaginative: lacking creativity or original ideas

    clumsy: lacking skill or grace; awkwardly done

    Where it sits · how original the thinking is

    clever → ingenious → groundbreaking

    groundbreaking vs. ingenious: Groundbreaking goes beyond ingenious: an ingenious solution is brilliantly clever within a known field, while a groundbreaking one is so original it opens territory no one has explored at all.

    clever vs. ingenious: Clever is a broader, more everyday word than ingenious -- clever can describe any quick or witty idea, while ingenious specifically emphasizes originality and inventive problem-solving.
