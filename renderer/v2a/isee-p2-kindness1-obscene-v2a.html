Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Obscene means so offensive or disgusting that it violates standards of decency.

See the word in context

The comedian's obscene jokes got the show moved to a late-night slot.

Reviewing the wall after vandals struck, the principal covers the obscene spray-painted images with tarps at once and tells reporters they were far too crude to show on camera.

Images too crude and offensive for public view are obscene. Obscene describes content -- words, pictures, gestures -- that offends or disgusts by violating standards of decency.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    lewd: Lewd means crude in a sexual, improper way -- a lewd remark or gesture. Obscene is broader and heavier: anything that offends or disgusts by violating standards of decency, sexual or not. A lewd joke is one kind of obscene content; gruesome images can be obscene without being lewd.

    coarse: Coarse means rough and unrefined -- coarse manners, coarse language. Coarse content is impolite but can still be shown in public. Obscene goes much further: it violates decency outright, the kind of material that gets covered up or banned rather than merely frowned at.

    graphic: Graphic means shown in vivid, explicit detail -- a graphic description leaves nothing out. Detail alone is not the offense: a medical textbook can be graphic and perfectly proper. Obscene means the content offends and disgusts by violating decency, whether or not it is detailed.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    decent: respectable and within accepted standards

    wholesome: good for everyone and offensive to no one

    Where it sits · how far past the line it goes

    vulgar → indecent → obscene

    vulgar vs. obscene: Vulgar is milder than obscene -- a vulgar joke is crude and tasteless, while an obscene one crosses the line into content that shocks and disgusts.

    indecent vs. obscene: Indecent marks the edge of the acceptable, while obscene sails past it: an indecent remark raises eyebrows, but an obscene image gets banned outright.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Obscene targets content: obscene language, obscene images, obscene gestures. Law and broadcasting lean on it heavily, since obscene material can be banned where merely rude material cannot. It also stretches to outrage of other kinds -- 'obscene profits' -- but the core is content that offends decency. For immoral conduct and motives, sordid is the sharper word.
