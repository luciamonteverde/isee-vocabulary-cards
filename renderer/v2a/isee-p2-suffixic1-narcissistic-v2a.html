narcissistic

nar-sih-SIS-tik -- stress on the third syllable, SIS.

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Narcissistic is having excessive love or admiration for oneself and demanding everyone else's admiration in return.

See the word in context

The narcissistic singer insisted that every photo from the charity concert show him and only him.

A narcissistic student joins the class party committee, but every meeting becomes a speech about her own brilliant ideas, and she takes credit for the decorations everyone else made.

The relentless self-focus treats every situation as a mirror. It goes beyond ordinary confidence: a narcissistic person is so in love with themselves that other people barely register.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    self-absorbed: Self-absorbed means so wrapped up in your own thoughts and concerns that you barely notice anyone else -- it can be inward and dreamy rather than boastful. Narcissistic is louder and harsher: a narcissistic person does not just overlook others, they demand everyone's admiration.

    egotistical: Egotistical means centering every conversation and situation on yourself at other people's expense -- an egotistical speaker turns every topic back to himself. Narcissistic points at the self-love underneath: a narcissistic person is so in love with themselves that admiration feels owed to them.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    humble: modest about one's own importance

    selfless: caring more for others than for oneself

    Where it sits · how extreme the self-obsession is

    vain → narcissistic → egomaniacal

    vain vs. narcissistic: Vain is lighter than narcissistic -- a vain person spends too long admiring the mirror, while a narcissistic person turns the whole world into one, expecting everyone's attention and admiration.

    egomaniacal vs. narcissistic: Egomaniacal pushes past narcissistic into outright obsession: a narcissistic person craves everyone's admiration, while an egomaniacal one is so consumed with self-worship that every plan and conversation must orbit them.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Narcissistic is a strongly disapproving word -- reserve it for self-love that has clearly gone too far, not for ordinary confidence or pride in a job well done. You may also meet it as a formal term in psychology, but in everyday writing it simply brands someone as excessively self-centered.
