epitome

ih-PIT-uh-mee -- FOUR syllables, stress on the second syllable, PIT. The ending is 'mee': never say 'EP-ih-tohm.'

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Epitome is a perfect example or embodiment of a quality.

See the word in context

With every tiny tool hung in its outlined place, the watchmaker's bench is the epitome of order.

Two hundred meters from the finish, the race leader hears a crash behind her -- her closest rival has fallen hard. She stops, runs back, and half-carries the injured girl across the line before crossing it herself, in last place. The next morning, every headline calls her the epitome of sportsmanship.

The runner is the epitome of sportsmanship: the quality itself, embodied at its absolute best. That is what epitome does -- it points at one person or thing and says: if you want to see this quality perfectly displayed, look here. The pattern is always 'the epitome of' plus the quality.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    essence: The essence of something is its purest core -- what remains once everything extra is stripped away. The epitome is not the core but the showcase: one real person or thing displaying the quality at its absolute best.

    ideal: An ideal is the perfect version as you imagine it -- the standard in your head that real things get measured against. The epitome is that standard caught in the flesh: a real example showing the quality at its very best.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    opposite: something completely contrary to a quality

    worst example: a case that shows a quality at its weakest

    Where it sits · how totally it stands for the quality

    embodiment → epitome → personification

    embodiment vs. epitome: Embodiment sits below epitome: calling a statue an embodiment of hope says the quality has taken concrete form, without ranking how well, while calling something the epitome of hope crowns it the finest display of the quality.

    personification vs. epitome: Personification is the strongest of the three: the epitome of patience shows the quality at its very best, but the personification of patience means the person and the quality have become impossible to tell apart.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Epitome travels in one pattern: The epitome of plus a quality -- the epitome of grace, of luxury, of tact. Keep it apart from its unit-mate archetype: the epitome is the finest embodiment of a quality, while the archetype is the original pattern later examples are modeled on. A hotel can be the epitome of luxury without any other hotel copying it; Sherlock Holmes is the archetype of the detective because every later sleuth descends from him. And mind the pronunciation: ih-PIT-uh-mee.
