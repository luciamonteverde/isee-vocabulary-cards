Quarantine

/KWAR-un-teen/ — rhymes with green verb

Hear It
What It Means

To quarantine someone or something is to keep them isolated from others for a period of time so that an illness cannot spread.

Show it in context

The new parrot spent two weeks in quarantine before joining the aviary.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

The hospital quarantined the patient suspected of carrying a highly contagious disease to prevent the virus from spreading to staff and other patients.

Quarantine is precise because it emphasizes both the isolation AND the specific reason — disease prevention. Plain 'isolated' would miss the health-control aspect. 'Quarantine' is the exact term for this specific medical practice of preventing disease spread.

Rootquarant- (Italian quaranta)forty (Italian, from Latin quadrāgintā ('forty'))

Quarantine comes from Italian quaranta giorni, 'forty days' — the length of time Venice made ships from plague-stricken ports wait offshore before anyone could land. Today it means any period of enforced isolation that keeps an illness from spreading.

Use the root in a new word: quarter

A quarter is one of four equal parts — the same Latin root for 'four' that grew into quaranta, 'forty,' the forty-day wait hiding inside quarantine.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

formal / medical

Quarantine is a formal, specific term for medical isolation to prevent disease spread. Use it when health authority and disease prevention are key. For general separation or isolation, the plainer word 'isolate' is more natural.
