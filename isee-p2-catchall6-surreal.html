Surreal

sur-REEL — literally 'beyond real'; a surreal moment feels like it's floated outside of reality entirely. adjective

Hear It
What It Means

Surreal is bizarre or unbelievable; having a strange, dreamlike quality.

Show it in context

Standing at the edge of the enormous canyon at sunrise, she said the view felt completely surreal.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

A young violinist who has practiced alone in her bedroom for ten years is suddenly invited to perform on stage with the exact orchestra she has listened to on recordings her whole life, and she can barely process that it's actually happening.

A moment so strange and dreamlike that it barely feels real, even while it's happening, is exactly what surreal means. Unlike 'exciting,' which is a feeling, surreal specifically describes something bizarre enough to seem unreal.

French sur- (above, beyond) combined with réel (real) to form surréel, coined for the Surrealist art movement of the 1920s. Something surreal, at its root, goes beyond ordinary reality into something dreamlike and bizarre.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it
