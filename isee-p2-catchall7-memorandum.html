Memorandum

mem-uh-RAN-dum — starts like 'memory'; a memorandum is written so people will remember it later. noun

Hear It
What It Means

Memorandum is a written record or note made for future use or reference.

Show it in context

The manager circulated a memorandum reminding employees about the new vacation policy.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

Before two companies officially merge, their lawyers draft and sign a short document laying out the basic terms both sides have agreed to, well before the lengthy final contract is ever written.

That short written record, meant to be referred back to later, is exactly what a memorandum is. Unlike a 'contract,' which is legally binding and final, a memorandum can be an informal or preliminary written note.

suffix-anduma thing that must be done (Latin (gerundive))

Latin memor meant 'mindful.' A memorandum is, at its root, 'a thing that must be remembered' -- literally a note written so something won't be forgotten.
